Up and Running in 3 Minutes

From sign-up to your first monitored endpoint — it's that quick.

1

Register & Add Your Endpoint

Sign up with email and username, paste your API URL, set your preferred check interval. That's it.

URL https://api.yourapp.com/health
Interval Every 60 seconds
2

We Ping. Continuously.

BullMQ workers hit your endpoint at the configured interval. Results stream live to your dashboard via Socket.io — no refresh needed.

DevPulse → ping → Your API → 200 OK → ✓ UP
3

Get Alerted Instantly

DOWN after 3 consecutive failures. Email + webhook fired immediately. Recovery notification follows when your API comes back up.

🔴 DOWN api.yourapp.com — 3 consecutive failures
🟢 UP api.yourapp.com — recovered after 4m 12s
