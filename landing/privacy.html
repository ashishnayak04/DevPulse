Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Data We Collect

To run endpoint monitoring we need to store two categories of information: what identifies you, and what your monitors produce.

  • Account data — your email address, username, and a securely hashed password (or the identity handed to us by Google/GitHub if you sign in with OAuth). We never see or store your raw password.
  • Monitoring data — the URLs of the endpoints you configure and the health-check results they generate: HTTP status codes, response times, up/down state transitions, and check timestamps.
  • Configuration — check intervals, alert thresholds, webhook URLs, and notification preferences you set for each endpoint.
  • Technical logs — standard server logs such as IP address, user agent, and request timestamps, used for security and abuse prevention.

We do not collect the response bodies of your monitored endpoints, and we do not read the traffic that passes between your services and their clients.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to operate the service you signed up for — nothing more:

  • Performing health checks on the endpoints you configure and streaming results to your dashboard in real time.
  • Sending transactional email: outage and recovery alerts, verification emails, password resets, and critical account notices.
  • Authenticating you and keeping your account secure.
  • Detecting and preventing abuse — for example, endpoints used to attack third-party systems.

We do not sell your data. We do not share monitoring results with anyone unless you explicitly publish them yourself via a public status page, and we do not use your endpoint data for advertising or profiling.

Data Retention

We keep data only as long as it is useful for the feature it powers:

  • Ping logs (individual check results) are retained according to the retention window of your plan — for example, 90 days on the free tier — after which they are permanently purged. Aggregate uptime statistics survive the purge; raw ping records do not.
  • Account and configuration data are retained for as long as your account is active.

When you request account deletion, all associated endpoints, ping logs, alerts, and status pages are removed in a cascade within 30 days. Some minimal records (e.g. invoice history or abuse-prevention entries) may be kept where legally required.

Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are in the EU/EEA or a jurisdiction with similar protections, you have the following rights over your personal data:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate account information directly from your settings page.
  • Erasure — delete your account and its data at any time.
  • Portability — export your endpoint configuration and monitoring history in a machine-readable format using the in-app Export feature.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@devpulse.io. We respond to verified requests within 30 days.

Cookies

We keep cookies deliberately minimal:

  • Essential cookies — an authentication session cookie so you stay logged in. The app cannot function without it; there is no way to opt out while using the dashboard.
  • Optional analytics cookies — if enabled, privacy-friendly aggregate analytics help us understand which landing pages are useful. They never identify you personally and can be declined without affecting any feature.

No advertising cookies, no cross-site trackers, no fingerprinting.

Third-Party Services

A small number of processors receive limited data strictly to provide the service:

  • Email delivery provider (SMTP) — sends transactional email (alerts, verification, password resets) on our behalf. Receives your email address and message content.
  • OAuth providers (Google, GitHub) — if you sign in with one of them, we receive only your identity identifier, email address, and display name. We never post to your accounts.
  • PagerDuty (optional) — only if you configure the integration yourself; alert payloads are then delivered to PagerDuty under their own privacy policy.

We do not otherwise disclose your data to third parties except where required by law.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or a data request? Reach us at support@devpulse.io.
